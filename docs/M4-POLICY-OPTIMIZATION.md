# OIE M4 — Policy Optimization & Calibration

M4 adds three production-oriented capabilities:

1. probability calibration independent of predictor training;
2. economic policy backtesting with intervention capacity constraints;
3. decision curves that expose the tradeoff between action volume and modeled net value.

The key principle is that **prediction quality and decision quality are different problems**. A well-ranked risk score may still be economically poor if interventions are expensive, operational capacity is limited, or its probabilities are badly calibrated.

Synthetic results are engineering validation only and must not be represented as customer ROI evidence.
